# AAUP admissions and registration test corpus

This is a concise, paraphrased test corpus collected from official Arab American University of Palestine (AAUP) pages and regulations. It is not itself an official regulation and must not override the cited source. Sources were checked on 2026-11-07. Admission thresholds and calendars can change; confirm the latest version before relying on them.

## Bachelor's registration load

Source: AAUP, *Instructions for Granting the Bachelor's Degree*, Article 8, printed pages 3-4. The official PDF is dated 2026-07-29:
https://www.aaup.edu/sites/default/files/2026-07/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D9%85%D9%86%D8%AD%20%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%AC%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%88%D8%B3%20-%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%83%D9%8A%D8%A9%20-%2029-07-2026.pdf

- For the ordinary semester, the general minimum is 12 credit hours and the maximum is 19 (Arabic: الحد الأعلى للفصل الاعتيادي 19 ساعة معتمدة).
- With the dean's approval, a student may register below the general minimum, but not below 9 credit hours, subject to the stated exceptions.
- The summer-semester maximum is 10 credit hours.
- In the regular semester following an academic warning, the maximum is 16 credit hours.
- A student on the honor roll may register 3 extra credit hours in the following regular semester, under the conditions in the instructions.

Useful search terms: credit-hour load, ordinary semester, summer semester, academic warning, honor roll, العبء الدراسي، الساعات المعتمدة، الحد الأعلى للساعات المعتمدة، الفصل الاعتيادي، الفصل الصيفي، الإنذار الأكاديمي.

## Diploma-to-bachelor bridging

Source: AAUP, *Bridging Instructions for Diploma Certificate Holders to Bachelor's Degree*:
https://www.aaup.edu/ar/study/bridging-instructions-diploma-certificate-holders-bachelors-degree

The page states that the listed instructions apply to students registered in intermediate-diploma programs after 2023-06-15. It lists these minimums. Arabic retrieval phrasing: التجسير إلى الهندسة، معدل الدبلوم، الامتحان الشامل:

- Engineering and Pharmacy: diploma cumulative average at least 80%, comprehensive exam at least 80%, and any assigned remedial courses at least 75%.
- Nursing and Health Sciences: diploma average at least 70%, comprehensive exam at least 70%, and remedial courses at least 70%.
- Law: diploma average at least 75%, comprehensive exam at least 75%, and remedial courses at least 70%.
- Other specializations: diploma average at least 60%, comprehensive exam at least 60%, and remedial courses at least 70%.
- The page excludes Medicine and Dentistry from bridging. Diploma graduates or students registered before 2023-06-15 are directed to the Admissions and Registration Deanship for their applicable instructions.

## Changing a major

Source: AAUP, *Frequently Asked Questions*, answer to “How can a student transfer from one major to another?”:
https://www.aaup.edu/ar/e-services/faqs

The answer says a student requests a major change by submitting the transfer form to the Admissions and Registration Deanship at the beginning of the first or second semester, no later than the end of the add/drop period. The Deanship decides the request according to the applicable admissions and registration policy.

Useful search terms: change major, transfer form, add/drop, الانتقال من تخصص إلى آخر، طلب الانتقال، تغيير التخصص، نموذج الانتقال، بداية الفصل، فترة السحب والإضافة.
